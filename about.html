Mission

Mission Statement

The Society of Cannabis in Higher Education is dedicated to advancing knowledge, research, and responsible leadership in the evolving field of cannabis within academic environments. We strive to foster interdisciplinary collaboration, promote evidence-based education, and support the development of equitable policies that address the social, economic, and health impacts of cannabis. Through scholarship, dialogue, and community engagement, we empower educators, students, and professionals to shape informed, ethical, and innovative approaches to cannabis in higher education and society at large.

Who We Are

About The Society of Cannabis in Higher Education

Connecting educators, researchers, students, and professionals through scholarship, dialogue, and community engagement.

The Society of Cannabis in Higher Education is a collaborative academic community dedicated to advancing the study, understanding, and responsible integration of cannabis-related knowledge across higher education. Bringing together educators, researchers, students, and professionals, the Society serves as a hub for interdisciplinary dialogue and innovation in this rapidly evolving field.

Grounded in a commitment to evidence-based inquiry and ethical leadership, the Society promotes rigorous scholarship, inclusive education, and informed policy development. We explore the complex social, economic, legal, and health dimensions of cannabis, recognizing its broad impact on communities and institutions alike.

Through research initiatives, educational programming, and community engagement, the Society empowers its members to contribute meaningfully to the future of cannabis studies. We are especially committed to fostering equity, addressing historical disparities, and supporting diverse perspectives within the academic and professional landscape.

By cultivating collaboration and critical thinking, The Society of Cannabis in Higher Education aims to shape thoughtful, informed, and forward-looking approaches that benefit both higher education and society at large.
