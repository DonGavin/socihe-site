Our Mission

Pushing Cannabis Forward The Society of Cannabis in Higher Education is dedicated to advancing knowledge, research, and responsible leadership in the evolving field of cannabis within academic environments. Cannabis is the umberella for medical marijuana and hemp. We strive to foster interdisciplinary collaboration, promote evidence-based education, and support the development of equitable policies that address the social, economic, and health impacts of cannabis. Through scholarship, dialogue, and community engagement, we empower educators, students, and professionals to shape informed, ethical, and innovative approaches to cannabis in higher education and society at large
