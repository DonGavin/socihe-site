Why We Exist

Cannabis Education Is Expanding — Standards Are Not

Cannabis education is growing rapidly across colleges and universities, yet the field lacks the academic standards expected in nearly every other discipline. As new programs emerge, institutions often develop curricula independently, creating inconsistencies in educational quality, student outcomes, and workforce preparation.

The Professor Network was established to address this challenge before fragmentation becomes the norm. The network serves as a collaborative forum where educators from across disciplines work together to identify essential knowledge, competencies, and learning outcomes for cannabis and hemp education.

"Every student entering the cannabis and hemp workforce should have access to a high-quality education built upon shared standards, interdisciplinary expertise, and a commitment to academic excellence." — Professor Network Vision
1

Interdisciplinary Strength

We connect medical faculty with business professors, chemists with communicators, agricultural scientists with public health experts — enabling a more comprehensive understanding of the industry and stronger educational standards.

2

Gap Analysis & Curriculum Standardization

The Professor Network fills a critical gap by providing a mechanism for program review, gap analysis, and curriculum standardization. Members identify emerging workforce needs, evaluate educational content, and help institutions build academically rigorous programs.

3

Neutral, Professor-Led Environment

By fostering evidence-based dialogue and scholarly collaboration, the Professor Network bridges higher education, industry stakeholders, healthcare professionals, policymakers, and the public — grounded in academic integrity throughout.

Standards Development

The Standards Development pillar drives a consensus-based process to develop curriculum standards for each focus area and its respective subtopics. It functions as the integrating spine of the Professor Network, drawing expertise from all three pillars to establish shared benchmarks for cannabis and hemp education.

How It Works

Network Structure & Participation

The Professor Network operates on a flexible participation model that allows professors to engage at varying levels of commitment — from open participation to active standards leadership. No formal membership required to begin.

General Participation

Open to all higher education professionals — no formal membership required

  • ✓ Participate in the Professor Network without formal membership status
  • ✓ Select a primary pillar reflecting your area of expertise
  • ✓ Voting rights during standard reviews within your primary pillar
  • ✓ Join discussions in additional pillars as an observer
  • ✓ Attend the Annual Convening regardless of membership status
How Voting Rights Work
Primary Pillar → Full voting rights on curriculum standards
Additional Pillars → Discussion & observation only — no voting

Standards Liaisons

Highly active leadership role — requires Standards Development as primary pillar

  • ✓ Manage focus area activities supporting the standards development process
  • ✓ Coordinate curriculum review cycles and facilitate consensus among participants
  • ✓ Serve as primary point of contact for their identified focus area
  • ✓ Lead agenda setting for the upcoming standards development cycle
  • ✓ Elected at the Annual Convening by network participants
Eligibility note: Standards Liaison eligibility may be restricted to formal network members. See membership phases below for details.
Formal Membership Benefits
  • Access to pillar collaboration boards — share course documents, troubleshoot curriculum challenges, and access peer-contributed materials
  • Free or reduced-cost access to The Society of Cannabis in Higher Education journal issues
  • Priority consideration for Standards Liaison roles (eligibility under review)
Membership Phases

Network Membership Rollout

Membership will be introduced in three sequential phases to ensure strong foundational governance before opening to the broader higher education community.

I

Founding Fellows

Onboard a foundational cohort of cannabis professors whose expertise serves as initial network dues. Focus: establishing governance bylaws, populating the resource library, and setting initial consensus benchmarks.

● Currently Active
II

Lifetime Fellows

Founding Fellows transition into permanent Lifetime Fellows with reduced ongoing membership fees, retaining all founding benefits and governance rights within the network.

○ Upcoming
III

General Membership

Open general network membership to all higher education instructors working in cannabis and hemp-related fields. Full access to all pillar collaboration tools and resources.

○ Upcoming

Annual Gathering

The Professor Network Annual Convening

The Professor Network holds an annual convening open to all participating professors, regardless of membership status. It serves as the primary forum for collective knowledge-sharing and network governance.

The convening is where the network sets direction — presentations from member institutions drive the agenda for the upcoming standards development cycle, ensuring the process remains grounded in real program experience.

Who Should Attend
  • Faculty and instructors in cannabis or hemp-related academic programs
  • Researchers working on cannabinoid science, policy, or public health
  • Curriculum developers and program directors at accredited institutions
  • Standards Development pillar members and Standards Liaison candidates

Annual Convening Agenda

Open to all participating professors · Held annually

  • 📊
    Emerging Trends & Program Successes

    Presentations on developments, issues, and institutional success stories from participant institutions across all three pillars.

  • 📋
    Standards Development Agenda Setting

    Informed by presented materials, the network collectively sets priorities for the upcoming curriculum standards development cycle.

  • 🔍
    Curriculum Standards Review

    Presentation and discussion of proposed or revised curriculum standards across focus areas — voting rights apply by pillar.

  • 🗳️
    Standards Liaison Elections

    Where applicable, members elect Standards Liaison representatives for each focus area to lead the upcoming review cycle.
